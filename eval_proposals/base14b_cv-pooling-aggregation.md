Core idea: Introduce a **Learned Spatial Attention-Guided Pooling** that dynamically generates channel-specific spatial weights via a lightweight 1x1 convolutional subnet, then applies these weights to the feature map before standard global average pooling. The attention subnet learns to emphasize discriminative spatial regions per channel, adapting to varying spatial resolutions and architectures.

Non-trivial crux: Designing the attention subnet to be **resolution-agnostic** (works for 1×1, 8×8, etc.) while avoiding overfitting to task-specific spatial patterns without access to labels. This is achieved by constraining the subnet to output spatial weights with **learned normalization** (e.g., softmax over spatial dimensions) and **parameter sharing** across channels, ensuring it learns generalizable spatial importance maps rather than memorizing class-specific activation patterns.
