Core idea: Introduce a **density-adaptive spectral clustering** approach where the similarity matrix is constructed using a custom distance metric that dynamically scales Euclidean distances by the harmonic mean of local densities at each point. This allows spectral clustering to handle non-convex shapes while mitigating density variations through locally normalized distances.

Non-trivial crux: Designing a **harmonic density-weighted distance** $ d'(x_i, x_j) = \frac{d(x_i, x_j)}{\min(\rho_i, \rho_j)} $, where $ \rho_i $ is the local density (inverse of average k-nearest-neighbor distance). This ensures points in sparse regions are prioritized for clustering separation, while dense regions allow tighter groupings. The harmonic mean prevents over- or under-estimation of distance in extreme density contrasts, enabling robustness to varied-density datasets without explicit parameter tuning.
